...

16    0x1000  pll drp [7:0] write data, [15:8] write addr
17    0x1004  pll drp [7:0] read  data, [15:8] read addr
18    0x1008  pll lock  [0] pll lock status
19    0x100c  pll reset and clk out control:
                        [0] clk out 0 enable
                        [8] pll reset
